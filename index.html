Our Story

Real Food. Real Flame. No Shortcuts.

At Ember & Bone we've made a simple promise: every burger is hand-pressed, every rack of ribs is slow-cooked over real hardwood, and every plate that leaves our kitchen is something we're genuinely proud of.

We've spent twelve years perfecting the art of the grill. Not chasing food trends. Not designing Instagram plates. Just cooking with honesty, heat and a deep respect for quality ingredients sourced from South African farmers we trust.
